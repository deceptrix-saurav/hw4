# Problem 10 — Storefront design

Campus Customs uses a dark ink canvas, Yale-inspired blue gradients, and bright pink accents for a distinctive black-and-pink course default. Playfair Display gives headlines a premium editorial feel while DM Sans keeps product text readable. Product cards lift on hover, the hero has a soft orb mark, and the floating concierge keeps help available without covering the catalog. Responsive breakpoints preserve the shopping hierarchy on narrow screens.
